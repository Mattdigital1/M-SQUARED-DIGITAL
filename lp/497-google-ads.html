Common Questions

Real questions from contractors before they start running ads.

What is the ad spend budget separate from the management fee?

The $497 (and $650 after) is our management fee — what you pay us. Ad spend is the budget you put directly into Google Ads so your ads actually show. You’ll need at least $500/month in ad spend, separate from our fee.

How fast will I start seeing leads?

Most clients start seeing calls within the first week of going live. Full optimization takes 30–60 days as we gather data on what’s converting in your specific market. We set realistic expectations upfront — no overpromising.

Do I need a website first?

Yes — Google Ads needs somewhere to send traffic. If you don’t have a website yet, check out our $297 website offer. We can build both at the same time and have you running ads and live within the same week.

What if I already tried Google Ads and it didn’t work?

That’s actually the most common situation we see. Most contractors who “tried Google Ads” ran them without proper keyword targeting, negative keywords, or call tracking. We rebuild from scratch using a proven local service framework. Different approach, different results.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. After the first month, it’s $650/month with no lock-in. We earn your business every single month. You can cancel any time with 30 days’ notice. We only ask for 60 days to show real results before making a call on fit.

What happens to my Google Ads account if I leave?

It’s yours. We set up campaigns under your Google account, not ours. If you ever leave, you take everything with you — the account, the data, the campaigns, and the history.
