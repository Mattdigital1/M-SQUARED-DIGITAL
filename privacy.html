Privacy Policy

How M Squared Digital Agency collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: May 19, 2026

M Squared Digital Agency ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website at msquareddigitalagency.com or submit any form on our site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a contact form, request a strategy call, or apply for any of our offers. This includes:

  • Full name
  • Business name and industry
  • Email address
  • Phone number (including mobile number)
  • Service area or location
  • Budget and service interests
  • Any other details you include in a message or form field

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our site, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This data is collected through cookies and standard web analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up on your request
  • Schedule and conduct strategy calls or consultations
  • Send service proposals and project updates
  • Communicate with you about your account or project
  • Send text messages and calls if you have opted in (see SMS section below)
  • Improve our website and services

SMS & Text Message Communications

By providing your mobile phone number and checking the SMS consent box on any of our forms, you consent to receive text messages (SMS/MMS) from M Squared Digital Agency at the phone number you provided, including messages sent by autodialer.

SMS opt-in occurs on the following pages:
• msquareddigitalagency.com/contact.html
• msquareddigitalagency.com/lp/297-website.html
• msquareddigitalagency.com/lp/497-google-ads.html

  • Message types: Appointment reminders, follow-up on your inquiry, project updates, and occasional promotional messages about our services.
  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies depending on your engagement. You may receive up to 4 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates: Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.
  • How to opt out: Reply STOP to any text message at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
  • Help: Reply HELP to any text message for assistance, or email us at william@m2squareddigital.com.
  • Consent is not required: Providing consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing any service from us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use tools like GoHighLevel (CRM), Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, and Calendly to operate our business. These platforms process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our company or others.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Contact form submissions and lead records are typically retained for up to 3 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to analyze traffic and improve user experience. We use Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, which set cookies on your device. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at william@m2squareddigital.com.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (such as Calendly, Facebook, or LinkedIn). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
